Drag furniture to move it · empty space + drag to orbit · pinch to zoom
Tap a piece of furniture in the scene (or add one) to edit it.
Outline:
Rooms: "+ Add room" draws a new one in Plan view; it snaps one "wall between rooms" away from the others, and facing walls up to 60 cm apart become one shared wall. Plan view: drag a corner or a wall, click a wall's length to type it, double-click a wall to add a corner (two corners + drag the piece between = a notch for a column). Select a wall + "Lock wall" to keep its length while you change the others (it slides along with them instead of stretching). "Lock room" freezes a finished room's outline and ceiling. Lengths are inside measurements; snap uses the Snap setting. Doors and windows snap to the nearest wall when you drag them close. Furniture turns red-outlined when it overlaps something.
Save up to three arrangements and flip between them to compare.
